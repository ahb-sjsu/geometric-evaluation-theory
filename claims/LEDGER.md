# GET claim ledger

One row per headline claim. Classes follow the observation-theory ledger: `[proved]`, `[demonstrated]`, `[replicated]`, `[predicted]`, `[exploratory]`, `[refuted]`, `[posited]`, `[open]`. A class is raised only by a sealed pass and lowered by a sealed miss. Every row names where the claim is stated and, once one exists, the gate and result that grade it. Machine-check names are filled at G1. Lean files live in `lean/GET/`, built on Atlas against Mathlib v4.32.2 with no `sorry`; the axiom audit is `lean/Axioms.lean`, and at the clean pass of 2026-09-07 every audited theorem depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
