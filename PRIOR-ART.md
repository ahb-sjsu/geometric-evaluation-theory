# Prior art

Swept 2026-09-07 (web search plus the program's own bibliographies). Each row names a neighbor, what it already contains, and what GET adds to it. A neighbor is credited in the paper wherever its result is used.

| Neighbor | What it already has | What GET adds |
|---|---|---|
| Ideal-point models. Not found in the sweep: any theory in which a resolution budget on consequences, as distinct from an information cost on states, is the source of a semiorder threshold, of preference reversal between fixed actions, or of a hull law. That absence is a claim about the sweep, not about the literature, and the sweep should be repeated before submission.
